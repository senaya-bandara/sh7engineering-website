Collaborative Robots (Cobot Robots)

Safe, flexible, human-friendly robots designed to work alongside operators.

Collaborative robots (cobots) are engineered with built-in safety features, force-limiting technology, lightweight structures, and easy programming. They require minimal guarding and are ideal for small factories, precise assembly work, machine tending, packaging, palletizing, and quality inspection. Cobots help businesses automate quickly without complex installation.

Brands: Universal Robots (UR), HikRobot, ABB YuMi, Aubo Robotics, Jaka Robotics, Doosan Robotics, Hanwha HCR

SCARA Robots (Selective Compliance Articulated Robot Arm)

High-speed SCARA robots built for fast pick-and-place, assembly, and precision operations.

SCARA robots specialize in high-speed horizontal movement, making them perfect for electronics assembly, packaging lines, sorting operations, screw driving, inspections, and repetitive handling tasks. They offer exceptional accuracy, compact size, and the fastest cycle times among robotic arm types.

Brands: Omron, Adept, Epson, Fanuc, Delta Electronics, Techman Robot

Articulated Robotic Arms (Trending & Most Common Type)

Flexible multi-axis robotic arms designed for welding, painting, material handling, and complex automation.

Articulated robots (4–7 axis) offer the highest versatility in motion. They perform tasks such as welding, palletizing, polishing, machining, and 3D handling in harsh or fast-paced environments. Their strong build, long reach, and advanced servo control make them highly reliable for heavy-duty industrial operations.

Brands: KUKA, Fanuc, ABB, Yaskawa Motoman, Kawasaki Robotics

Delta Robots (High-Speed Parallel Robots – Trending in Packaging)

Ultra-fast delta robots ideal for picking, sorting, packaging, and high-speed lightweight handling.

Delta robots use a triangular parallel-arm structure to achieve extremely high speeds with precision. They are commonly used in the food industry, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and FMCG packaging lines where fast and accurate sorting or placing is required.

Brands: ABB FlexPicker, Fanuc M-1iA, Delta Electronics, Codian Robotics, HikRobot
