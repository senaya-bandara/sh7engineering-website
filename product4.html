Photoelectric Sensors

Reliable non-contact object detection for any industrial environment.

Photoelectric sensors use light beams to detect the presence, absence, or distance of an object with high accuracy. They offer long sensing ranges, fast response times, and excellent performance even in dusty or harsh conditions, making them ideal for packaging, assembly lines, conveyor systems, and automated inspection. These sensors are essential for precise object detection in modern automation.

Through-Beam Sensors

Maximum detection accuracy using separate emitter and receiver units.

Through-beam sensors provide the highest reliability among photoelectric sensing types by using an emitter and receiver positioned opposite each other. They offer long-distance detection, outstanding immunity to ambient light, and superior performance with transparent or fast-moving objects. Ideal for packaging, bottling lines, counting applications, and safety detection.

Reflective Sensors (Retro-Reflective)

Efficient detection using a reflector for long-range, reliable sensing.

Reflective sensors operate by sending a beam of light toward a reflector and detecting interruptions caused by an object. They are easy to install, offer long sensing distances, and work well with irregularly shaped items. Ideal for conveyor lines, object counting, packaging automation, and general presence sensing.
