Sensors

Selected sensor types available through SH7 Engineering Solutions. Details and models can be customized based on your application.

Photoelectric Sensors

Reliable object detection over longer ranges

Ideal for conveyor systems, packaging lines, and presence detection where non-contact, fast response, and stable sensing are critical. Available in diffuse, through-beam, and retro-reflective types.

Inductive Sensors

Non-contact metal detection for harsh environments

Rugged sensors for position and presence detection of metal targets in automation, packaging, and assembly lines. Available in various sizes, sensing distances, and output types.

Through-Beam Sensors

High reliability for long-distance detection

Separate emitter and receiver design for robust object detection in dusty, humid, or high-speed conveyor applications. Ideal for counting, presence detection, and safety-related triggering.

Reflective Sensors

Compact detection with reflector-based setup

Retro-reflective sensors with mirror/reflector for easy installation and alignment. Suitable for object detection on conveyors, packaging, and warehouse automation.

Fiber Optic Sensors

Flexible sensing for tight and high-temperature spaces

Fiber-based sensing heads for miniature detection points, curved paths, and high-temperature zones. Ideal for electronics, packaging, and small-part detection tasks.

Background Suppression Sensors

Stable detection independent of background

Sensors designed to detect the object while ignoring background surfaces, ideal for presence detection on variable or shiny conveyors and fixtures.

Color Sensors

Color-based detection and sorting

Sensors that detect color differences for label checking, print mark detection, product sorting, and quality control applications.
