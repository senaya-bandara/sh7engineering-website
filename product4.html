Photoelectric Sensors

Lightweight, versatile detection for any line

Compact, reliable sensing for conveyors, packaging, and material handling. From clear-object detection to long-range presence control, our photoelectric range helps keep your machines running with precise, consistent feedback.

Other Sensor Types

We support multiple sensing principles for different applications and environments.

Inductive Sensors

Non-contact metal detection for harsh environments

Rugged proximity sensors for position feedback, end-of-stroke detection, and metal part counting. Available in cylindrical and rectangular bodies with flush and non-flush options.

Through-Beam Sensors

Long-distance, high-reliability detection

Separate emitter–receiver pairs for high sensing distances and reliable detection in dusty or contaminated environments – ideal for packaging and pallet lines.

Reflective Sensors

Reliable label, mark, and object detection

Retro-reflective and background-suppression types for cartons, labels, and small parts. Excellent for conveyor applications where space is limited.

Fiber Optic Sensors

Flexible sensing for tight or hot areas

Compact fiber heads with remote amplifiers for high-temperature zones, cramped machines, and applications where standard housings cannot fit.

Background Suppression Sensors

Stable detection independent of background

Detect objects reliably even when the conveyor, machinery, or background colour changes. Useful for mixed-product lines and packaging machines.

Color Sensors

Precise colour and mark detection

Dedicated sensors for print marks, packaging artwork, and colour-based sorting. Ideal for FMCG, labelling, and high-speed packaging lines.
